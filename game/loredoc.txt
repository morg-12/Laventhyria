Skorgmiloquis monarchy and early Laventhyria:
The Skorgmiloquis regime was the old monarchy that ruled over what is now Laventhyria. Though they were popular when they came to power, long wars, high taxes, tariffs, conscription, and demanding excessive tribute of their vassals turned the nobility and the common people alike against them.
The Levant family was a wealthy merchant house at the time. In the revolt against the house of Skorg-Miloquis and their regime, the Levants ended their trade with loyalists to the house and increased trading with the revolutionaries, securing them a seat at the Assembly after the regime's fall.
They suggested that the new nation be called Levanthyria, from Levant meaning "rising" in the Hyrian tongue. The Assembly was intrigued, deciding to use this as the name of the new currency, but felt that the Levants were trying to tie the new nation to them.
Thus came about the name of Laventhyria.
The Levants, being a powerful merchant family, grew in wealth and influence as the years went on. They used their control over Laventhyria's supplies to influence the Assembly into passing laws that were beneficial to their bottom line.
Eventually, they had gained so much wealth and influence that they felt they could rule better themselves. 
Suddenly, Assembly members began falling over dead left and right. A count here, a mayor there, a duchess here, a governor there. All the while, the house of Levant stood unharmed.
It is rumored and widely believed that the Levants were behind this, but their descendants deny any such allegations.
Declaring a state of emergency, the Levants quickly consolidated power under themselves. Technically, the Levanthyrian state is still in a state of emergency, and the position of the Levants comes only from their status as the sole members of the Assembly.
However, they have modified the constitution to allow them to rule as monarchs and establish a system of subordinates who can rule over the lands, known as the nobility.
