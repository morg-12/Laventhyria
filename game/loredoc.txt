Skorgmiloquis monarchs:
The Skorgmiloquis regime was the old monarchy that ruled over what is now Laventhyria. Though they were popular when they came to power, long wars, high taxes, tariffs, conscription, and demanding excessive tribute of their vassals turned the nobility and the common people alike against them.
The Levant family was a wealthy merchant house at the time. In the revolt against the house of Skorg-Miloquis and their regime, the Levants ended their trade with loyalists to the house and increased trading with the revolutionaries, securing them a seat at the Assembly after the regime's fall.
